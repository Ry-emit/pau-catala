About Pau

A rider built
by the hardest phase.

Pau Català Sanuy first rode at a summer camp on a riding-school farm near Barcelona — and never really got off. Thirteen years later, eventing is everything: the precision of dressage, the courage of cross-country and the nerve of show jumping, all held together in one partnership.

His turning point came riding with Eduardo Vía Dufresne, who took him to two European Championships — and a silver medal. He sharpened everything in England under Lizzie & Eric Winter, who pushed his riding to the next level. Today he competes at CCI3*, based in Lübz, Germany, training with international eventer Malin Hansen.

His method is simple and personal: the horse comes first. Every horse gets its own plan, built on patience and trust, until it believes it can jump anything — and would go to the end of the world with you.

“Give a horse confidence, and it will go to the end of the world with you.”— Pau Català Sanuy

The three phases

The complete horseman — three phases, one rider.

Every eventer lives or dies by all three. Here’s what each one tests, and where Pau is strong.

01Dressage

Precision, obedience, harmony — the compulsory figures of the sport.

Pau’s edge

Calm, accurate work built on relaxation — the foundation Pau lays in every young horse.

02Cross-Country

Courage, fitness and judgment of pace over solid fences and terrain.

Pau’s edge

The heart of the sport — bold, confident and clear across country.

03Show Jumping

Nerve and accuracy with a tired horse, the day after cross-country.

Pau’s edge

Careful and rhythmic — keeping the partnership together on the final day.
